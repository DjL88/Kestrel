# Kestrel — from good to outstanding
**Owner direction: 21 November 2026 · Programme charter + executable roadmap**

> **Purpose:** Build the most enjoyable, trustworthy complete *authentic photographic* workflow. Wildlife is our demanding image-quality proving ground, **not** an exclusion of portraits, landscape, travel, family, sports, macro or other genres. Depth over breadth. Simplicity when wanted, precision when needed.

This is the **current cross-workstream delivery programme**. [PRODUCT-SPEC.md](PRODUCT-SPEC.md) and [PRODUCT-PHILOSOPHY.md](PRODUCT-PHILOSOPHY.md) remain binding product contracts. [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md) tracks implementation and evidence; [issue #418](https://github.com/DjL88/Kestrel/issues/418) is the shared taskboard. **This programme takes priority over historic task ordering when priorities differ**; do not erase remaining tasks or manufacture completion.

## 0. What success means

A person can take 500 actual mixed RAW/JPEG/HEIC photos from a camera card, folder, phone or NAS; safely import/relink; find their best frames; improve them naturally; batch-match a consistent look; inspect subjects at pixel detail; finish captions/copyright/colour and export at the promised full quality; then reopen entirely offline with **no lost edits or external RAW/denoise/export app**. The simplest journey takes a few understandable choices; every decision stays reversible. Source images never change. Advanced users can inspect profiles, masks, curves, output sharpening, metadata, colour spaces and export settings without a maze of duplicated controls.

### Hard constraints
1. **No generative scene editing.** No invented/replaced objects, hallucinated feathers, generative fill, altered wildlife facts or model-written image pixels. Local intelligence can recommend reversible settings/crops, analyse subjects and produce accurate editable masks. Ordinary physically grounded RAW development/denoise/interpolation remains permitted with empirical artefact guardrails; no unsupported claims of 'true' detail reconstruction. **An optional text assistant may explain/edit sliders, not paint the photo.**
2. **Offline is complete.** No login/network required for free import, culling, local models, RAW development, editing, export, reopening or recovery. Optional BYO remote services and online licence purchase/sync must fail without locking local work. No hidden image upload.
3. **Free is genuinely useful.** Free includes authentic RAW and raster develop, local cull, manual edit, reasonable presets, local backup/relink, original-size high-quality ordinary output, accessibility and privacy. Never artificially degrade image quality, inject watermarks, or make access to originals/edits contingent on payment. Pro earns its value from *advanced automation and throughput*, deeper workflow, advanced masks/batch intelligence/export styles and selected premium Kestrel-owned local models, not by withholding ordinary image correctness.
4. **Two editions only: Free / Pro.** Major-version entitlement is explicit (e.g. Pro v1 stays usable offline indefinitely; Pro v2 is a new optional paid major upgrade). Minor and security/bug-fix releases for a licensed major version are included; define support windows before selling. Never surprise-disable an installed major release. No recurring subscription is required; optional third-party BYO AI usage is user-funded and opt-in. Keep licence validation signed and offline-resilient, not a continuous cloud dependency.
5. **Proprietary destination, legal provenance today.** Own the Kestrel rendering pipeline, proprietary algorithms, dataset rights, training recipes, weights and UX progressively. Do **not** assume a clean-room reimplementation avoids relevant patents, codec licences, trade secrets or dataset rights. Maintain an SBOM and component/weight/codec rights register including licence, attribution, patent flags, commercial rights, replacement plan and counsel decision. Do not copy code, binary internals, model weights, proprietary output datasets or protected trade secrets. Independent interface testing, documented standards and independent mathematical implementation may be lawful in defined circumstances, but require specific UK/EU/US legal review before distribution. Nikon HE/HE* and JPEG XS / TicoRAW require a **separate patent/distribution clearance gate**. Do not use other vendors' trained model outputs to train a replacement without appropriate permissions.
6. **No false completion.** Distinguish DESIGNED, CODED, MERGED, TESTED, VERIFIED-ON-DEVICE and BLOCKED. Lack of GitHub Actions billing is NOT a passing test. Do not mark an unrun acceptance test green or pretend a planned local model is already commercially trained.
7. **One app / one core.** No divergent mobile editor. Progressive adaptive UX with common photo identity, recipe, processing stages, zoom, crop and export truth across Windows/macOS/iOS/Android/web. Browser may have declared resource limits; don't silently reduce requested output.

## 2. The five end-to-end workstreams

### WORKSTREAM 1 — The complete photographer journey / integration lead
**Accountable outcome:** A photographer finishes a shoot in Kestrel without procedural dead ends, missing originals, context-switching or an external export tool.
- Own cross-module **one photo identity/recipe/task engine**; progressive ingest from camera card/folders/phone/NAS (source hashes, RAW+JPEG pairing, dedupe, safe optional second backup), linked originals, transactional catalogue/relink/reopen, persistent edit history and virtual versions.
- Own a single unified batch queue used for background processing/import/analysis/export, bounded CPU/RAM, cancel/retry/resume after crash/restart, permissions reacquisition, atomic collision-safe output receipts, truthful job states and errors.
- Deliver a single **Export Flight**: original-copy vs rendered master are unmistakable; PNG/JPEG/ICC 16-bit TIFF where proven, presets, crop/resize/watermark/text/opt-in metadata, fixed proof from same renderer, one-click destination, accurate full-size profiles. Never pass 8-bit JPEG preview off as a RAW editing master.
- Integrate contracts produced by other workers, coordinating ownership of shared App/PhotoEditor files. Feature must land in **user path**, not merely a library utility.
- Shipping acceptance: 500-source scenario, offline reopen + restart/retry, zero wrong-photo/no silent quality fallback, exported proof matches output; separate VERIFIED or NOT RUN labels.

### WORKSTREAM 2 — Kestrel imaging science / RAW quality lead
**Accountable outcome:** Beautiful, colour-faithful natural photographs throughout the real editor, not just in a raw benchmark command.
- Own sensor-source provenance and the real **continuous high-bit-depth working pipeline** through demosaic, WB, camera/input ICC, linear colour, highlight recovery, lens profiles, edits, masks, denoise/sharpen, output transform and ICC high-bit masters. Make previews and exports consistent. Prioritise Nikon Z8/Z5II including lossless and *legally supported* HE/HE*; no invented decoder success.
- Fix the known 8-bit JPEG intermediate in the normal editable RAW journey. No '16-bit TIFF' success just for packaging 8-bit data in 16-bit samples. Same pixel math across devices or explicit honest capability fallback.
- **TrueDetail**: physical/sensor-aware classical baselines; develop Kestrel-owned denoise/demosaic networks only against consented/licensed RAW pairs and calibrated camera/ISO noise. Preserve feather barbs, bokeh, skin, architecture, landscapes, night sky, fine text. Measure at 100% and final print/export resolution, blind compare against NX Studio, DxO and Adobe.
- Batch look-matching must use this same scene-referred renderer; define reversible per-photo tone/colour matching contract with UX worker.
- Legal stop: HE/HE* codec patents/commercial permission unresolved ⇒ no shipping claim, explicit support matrix; alternate truthful source if allowed, no surreptitious patent-risk decoder inclusion.
- Shipping acceptance: real-camera traces + independent visual/colour/ICC high bit tests and lens/ISO benchmark; test statuses truthful.

### WORKSTREAM 3 — Kestrel Select & understanding / culling lead
**Accountable outcome:** Fast, safe identification of the strongest photo and editable masks that respect the real scene.
- Build **Subject Detail inspector** contracts: subject/eye focus windows at true pixels, stable across A/B bursts; analyse eye/face/subject sharpness, motion vs background sharpness, poses/variety, duplicates and a clear 'needs review' class. Never autotrash uncertain keepers.
- Confidence-calibrated local subject detection across **wildlife, people/pets, aircraft, sport, landscapes, architecture and macro**; wildlife is primary benchmarking, not the only usable category. Separate species ID (often uncertainty) from generic subject segmentation; uncertain birds vs aircraft abstain.
- Deliver reliable accurate feather/leg/branch-aware reversible masks: subtract/add/intersect, feather, edge refinement, no hard leaks/halos; preview identical to high-bit export; light user correction rather than re-prompting the model endlessly.
- Maintain independent real-shoot labelled datasets and class-balanced negative examples; classify model confidence only when calibrated. Accept user corrections as *private opt-in training labels*, never automatically upload.
- Shipping acceptance: measured favourite-keeper recall/false rejection, pixel detail correctness, segmentation boundary scores and 500-photo foreground/background tests; abstention quality reported.

### WORKSTREAM 4 — Effortless excellence / UX and language lead
**Accountable outcome:** A beautiful, quiet, understandable **same workspace** that works on mouse, touchscreen and keyboard; sophisticated tools only when requested.
- Own shared interaction design: **Library → Review → Edit** one frame; consistent filmstrip/zoom/crop, editable/undockable/pinnable panels, user preferences, small restrained controls, plain British English, complete translations, accessibility, colourblind-safe cues, reduced motion; phone, tablet landscape and foldable layout parity.
- Design/integrate **Natural Auto** into a simple preview → Apply/Compare flow, with very short useful explanations and 'already good' abstention. No verbose internal model caveats or invented improvements; no silent change to manual edits.
- Build **Match This Look** as a coherent shoot-wide *reversible* recipe: copy visual intent from a confirmed reference while independently correcting tone/exposure/WB per photo, preview representative samples, exclusions/manual locks, strength slider, explicit undo. Use Kestrel-owned classical statistics first, optional local model later. Collaborate with imaging lead on core colour constraints and integration lead on batch.
- Build **Finish My Shoot**: one helpful queue of 'review uncertain picks / edit selects / fill metadata / export', without mandatory wizard/extra steps. Default sensible values and auto-skip single-choice UI; Advanced editing remains available.
- **Colour confidence**: exposure/clipping and neutral-colour hints, reference compare, accessible tones and understandable advanced tooltips; built-in beautiful restrained natural looks for diverse genres, no filters that falsify subjects.
- Shipping acceptance: new user's path from import to export, professional path with precision, A11y/translation/phone/tablet/desktop flows; no modal overflow, no forced repeated clicks.

### WORKSTREAM 5 — Kestrel-owned platform / architecture & release lead
**Accountable outcome:** An economical, safe, offline-native foundation for Free and Pro, local intelligence, provenance and installable releases.
- Own native runtime adapters and architecture: reliable local caches + source-folder recovery snapshots where writable; safe NAS/volume reconnect; encrypted OS-keychain BYO secrets; signed model update manifest, content-digest validation, version rollback, optional cloud sync only if explicitly chosen; no unannounced network use.
- Own local inference *adapters* (ONNX CPU/GPU/WebGPU, Core ML where supported, Android CPU/NNAPI where practical, deterministic non-ML fallback), memory/GPU budgets, model selection per device and signed package manifests; never claim inference providers/quality without real device testing.
- Own Kestrel model strategy and rights ledger: separate **Kestrel-owned model** experiments from third-party model evaluation; dataset provenance, subject/bird/perceptual diversity, consent, rights, retention/deletion, training/eval split, weight checksum, model card and quantised CPU fallback; create a reproducible training/evaluation pipeline, not a folder of hypothetical model names.
- Evaluate an **optional local text assistant** for answering natural-language edits (translate request into a bounded, reviewable slider/mask recipe; provide concise explanation); rules/classical engine before a heavyweight model; never generative photo manipulation, auto-execute untrusted text, or call the network without consent. On constrained devices, simple static help remains fully functional.
- Free/Pro signed entitlements per paid major version, offline grants and updates; prohibit always-online validation and lockout when licence service unavailable. Pro/Free have the same core photo fidelity and data portability. Low-cost cloud: static site/downloads, signed manifests, minimal licence/control plane; explicit unit cost budgets.
- Before promoting 'proprietary' rights, audit *existing public repository* and dependencies; a public repository makes source visible and existing third-party open licences persist. New proprietary modules/weights need correct rights, code permissions and suitable repository access.
- Optional conservation funding: propose transparent fixed donation per sale or audited share of net receipts with nominated eligible partners; no unapproved charity claims, payment/tax/legal counsel review before advertising.
- Shipping acceptance: reproducible signed packages, offline licences/restores, model provenance, keychain, resilience to disconnected cloud, documented cloud running costs and no unsafe telemetry.

## 3. What we build ourselves versus borrow, and in what order

**Tier A — own NOW:** edit recipe and rendering graph orchestration; user experience; metadata/identity/tasking; private culling/score aggregation logic; simple classical exposure/colour matching and local look profiles; scene-bound masking corrections and confidence thresholds; synthetic noise simulator and quality evaluation harness.

**Tier B — own progressively through experiments:** camera-aware demosaic/denoise, reversible tone and colour engines, proprietary compact subject/pose/mask models, lens/camera calibration datasets where legally collected. For each experiment use properly licensed own-captured RAW scenes with varied ISO/exposure, calibration targets, low/mixed light, moving subjects, fine feather/branch edges, portraits, landscapes, architecture, macro, night and known negatives. Publish internal reproducible benchmarks, never promise competitor parity.

**Tier C — temporarily integrate where lawful/practical:** platform RAW libraries and OS decode, ONNX inference runtime, image codecs, standards-based metadata/ICC and permissively licensed trained detection models *only* after licence and commercial/redistribution assessment. Track whether weights themselves have different terms from inference code and whether static/dynamic linking imposes obligations. Replace only where Kestrel-owned implementation clearly improves quality/control/cost or independence. Do not rebuild mature interoperability infrastructure merely to claim ownership.

**AI boundary:** *AI in development* is not *generative content in photographs*. Models may read/understand and select parameters; outputs to users are ordinary reversible image edits. Neural denoise and other learned reconstruction must be scrutinised for synthetic texture/hallucinations, particularly real-world wildlife documentary integrity. Report borderline techniques accurately; favour a conservative implementation.

**Training programme:** (1) acquire consented rights-cleared real RAW/ground truth and photographer-scored evaluation set; (2) baseline non-neural render/noise and camera-profile accuracy; (3) train small camera/ISO-aware residual/noise prediction model with faithful ground truth, architecture/model-data cards; (4) blind compare with no reference leakage across same burst/camera; (5) optimise quantisation, tiles, memory; (6) sign/version model pack and fallback; (7) broaden camera/genre coverage. Keep training compute separate from end-user app; never upload images by default. Paid tokens for research/training may accelerate work, **not** substitute for rights, verified data or good evaluations.

## 4A. Product architecture decision — local intelligence, signed data updates and optional compute (21 November 2026)

This is an **extension to the five existing workstreams**, not a sixth worker, a separate roadmap, or a mandate for a heavy backend. Implement the smallest complete version and measure device/cost/privacy impact before expanding. Where this section narrows a general earlier idea, use this section.

### A. Asset lifecycle: one curated backend, locally stored immutable packages

1. **Separate data from executable code.** Independently updatable data: ONNX/model weights, class labels/embeddings, small offline photography knowledge indexes, Lensfun and verified camera/lens optical profiles, camera compatibility *records*, nonexecutable LUTs/config. Executable native/WASM codec implementations, runtime binaries and app plugins require versioned **signed application/OS-store releases**, not arbitrary hot-loaded backend downloads. Nikon HE/HE* requires tested technical support **and** written commercial/patent/redistribution review; new LibRaw 202611 HE support is a research candidate, not automatic release permission.
2. **Curate upstream on a central low-frequency backend worker** (initially weekly; manually callable for urgent fixes): read only official and approved upstream releases, exact artefact digest, SPDX/SBOM/weight licence, provenance and model cards. Test against representative positive, negative and known-bad fixtures (camera/lens compatibility, correction geometry, ONNX I/O/runtime, hallucination, unsupported-platform). Quarantine untrusted assets. A human approves promotion if licensing/compatibility/safety changes. Release artefacts to **immutable versioned object storage**; never overwrite active URIs.
3. **One signed manifest** advertises per asset: logical id, release version, SHA-256, byte size, kind (lens-db/model/knowledge/camera-record), licence and attribution URL, training/data provenance, platform+architecture, minimum/maximum app and inference runtime versions, optional dependencies/linked label-index version, expected disk/RAM class, test/build evidence and rollout channel. Sign exact bytes with an offline-controlled Ed25519 release key or equivalent key custody; pin public verification keys in app. Keep emergency revocation and key rotation documented. Object storage and CDN are *distribution*, not authority.
4. **Client side:** open immediately using bundled or last verified local asset; check manifest occasionally online (max every 24h by default, jittered), not every photo; optionally allow manual Check now. Verify signature, anti-rollback/compatible manifest, size/quota, HTTPS origin allowlist, exact SHA, and smoke-test asset in isolated staging before atomically switching active pointer. Keep previous known-good and built-in fallback. After interruption, storage-full, interrupted network, corrupt or incompatible model, failed smoke test, or killed application, restore last good asset with a calm status. Never block editing, delete originals, or lose cached assets just because the publisher is offline.
5. **Download policy:** small lens/label/compatibility updates may silently update if user allows normal updates; substantial VLM/denoise/segmenter packs require a clear Wi-Fi/storage/metered choice and device suitability estimate. Limit cache budget and per-device concurrent downloads; resume chunk/range when supported; no background photo uploads and no identifiable EXIF in update checks. Version compatibility for saved edits matters: persist model and renderer/profiles versions alongside edit decisions when changes alter results.
6. **Low-cost implementation:** adapt existing \`docs/EQUIPMENT-UPDATES.md\`, \`src/providers/modelStore.ts\`, control-plane \`/v1/models/manifest\`, storage and existing signed-manifest PRs. Do not create a second updater, model store, manifest schema or admin portal. Use object storage/static hosting for public bytes, minimal authenticated control plane only when required. Cost ledger for storage, bandwidth, updates, inference, retries and support.

**First integrated acceptance:** kill app midway through model update, modify bytes on server, switch storage offline, corrupt label package, supply wrong architecture, fill disk, simulate rollback. Expected: no execution of untrusted artefact, old model/optics still work, photos stay local and source unchanged. All actual tests with status.

### B. Phone/tablet memory: solve locally before using cloud

Nikon Z9/Z8-class 45 MP HE/HE* decode can use substantial temporary memory (LibRaw November 2026 snapshot cites **about 400 MB intermediate** for Z9, *before* editable high-bit raster, GPU textures, masks and application overhead). The effective working set could be much larger; don't assume internet buffers solve local decode memory.

- **First:** show embedded/reliable preview immediately; original stays linked on SSD/camera card/NAS. Bound parallel RAW development to one heavyweight job; use input/output byte accounting, memory pressure feedback, device-class budgets and eviction of obsolete buffers, GPU resources and stale views; retain cached pyramid/tiles. Use half/quarter resolution preview only when clearly labelled, then render true requested full size through genuinely supported high precision paths. Cache partial results on source or device without silent compression. Support 45–60+ MP scenarios on capable tablets/phones with local/USB SSD where possible.
- **Second:** investigate decoder memory improvements honestly: tile downstream demosaic/mask/denoise/export, overlap/halo and streaming writes, scratch files/memory-mapped intermediates where permission/OS allows, lower-precision *working preview* while retaining high-precision final, format-specific raw decode limits. **Tiled post-decode processing does not eliminate a full-buffer codec decoder.** Build a resource estimator from pixel dimensions, codec variant, model/onnx resident memory, output channels and device available memory; refuse with explanation if unsafe, do not silently kill or substitute quality.
- **Third, only opt-in and online:** optional remote heavy-develop/export on user-controlled nearby desktop/NAS first, or ephemeral cloud job for accounts that enable it. Present estimated upload size/data charges, time, privacy and per-job cost/Pro entitlement; user explicitly selects photo(s) and destination. Originals remain local; upload a temporary copy only when explicitly approved, encrypted in transit and at rest, isolated by job/tenant, no provider training; use region preference (UK/EEA where viable), consent/versioned processing notice, lifecycle delete + purge job receipts. Server processing needs plaintext while executing: **do not market it as end-to-end encrypted**. Verify source digest and renderer version and return matching photo identity and checksum/provenance. Cloud failure must leave local edit/project intact; user can cancel, retry or continue offline. Set hard per-account quotas, job timeout/concurrency and spend caps; no unlimited free remote compute.
- **Measure demand, don't assume:** some high-resolution wildlife/sports photographers use iPads or phones with external SSDs in the field, for rapid culling, previews or even editing. Prioritise mobile *review* speed, then full quality on supported hardware, with cloud acceleration as a convenience rather than an excuse for weak offline functionality.

**First acceptance:** Z9/Z8 HE where rights/decoder allow; simulate <=4, 6, 8, 16 GB memory classes, offline+metered+airplane mode; repeat background/foreground and kill/reopen; accurate preview-to-export claims, high-precision output provenance, no OOM or losing original. Benchmarks must say PASS/FAIL/NOT RUN.

### C. Offline Photo Advisor — THREE real edits, not synthetic photographs

This is **depth in Natural Auto**, not a new standalone chatbot or generative image editor. Use existing photo metadata and deterministic analysis, with an optional small offline multimodal vision/language model (licence-checked, quantised, tested on constrained devices) *only* for scene interpretation, reasoning and concise explanations. The local AI proposes bounded **edit recipes**; only the existing Kestrel renderer changes pixels.

- From **one source image and untouched original**, compute an evidence set: lens/camera/source reliability, subject/eye location and confidence, luma/chroma histogram, clipped shadows/highlights, neutrals/white-balance uncertainty, noise versus fine texture, skin/feather/foliage sensitivity, mask quality. Abstain where unreliable and never label a generic visual guess as objective truth.
- Propose three reversible visual treatments, e.g. **Natural** (faithful), **Subject** (gentle local subject separation), **Creative** (tasteful but authentic). This is 3 rendered **previews of the same photograph**, never generated objects or replaced scene regions. The model may propose parameter ranges and mask ID/weight, not pixel arrays. Cap edits in a versioned allowlist and enforce colour/headroom/noise/halo constraints.
- **Best of three by region**: represent choices as a common global base exposure/WB/colour transform plus mask-specific treatment recipes (subject, sky/background, foreground), each attached to the *same untouched source*. Choose locally scored or user-approved adjustment strength for a region, use edge-aware feathered alpha and bounded mask intersections. **Never stitch separately rendered RGB images** with incompatible local white balance or tone, fake texture, broken lighting or seams. Evaluate combined result for clipping, mask seams, colour discontinuity, unnatural saturation and detail damage. Where uncertain, fall back to the Natural option.
- UI: minimal 'Natural | Subject | Creative' selectable previews and 'Refine/Apply', optional 'Combine best adjustments' preview with fine manual control; optional section comparison and concise 'why'. *Do not* offer this as a mandatory extra wizard or replace the manual editor. Preserve individual manual edit locks; always undo and reset; keep one coherent before/after and high-bit export renderer. A no-change suggestion is a valid outcome. On mobile use compact previews, not three simultaneous full-size renders.
- Model-free behaviour is a usable default. Run 3 low-resolution recipe render previews on bounded background queue, lazily show detail at requested zoom; no compulsory VLM download. Do not imply AI knows 'everything' about photography. Use locally authored knowledge on colour, bird/animal ethics and genre-specific handling; no copying copyrighted tutorials into training corpus.
- **Acceptance:** natural/portrait/landscape/bird/aircraft/night/human skin fixtures, tricky masks and backgrounds, rejected model inputs, 100% detail, colour and ICC, exact same selected recipe on export, no invented content, no auto-application without user approval. Blind preference tests of three options *and* combined treatment versus no edit.

### D. Optional consented Pro Photographer Learning Panel (no default upload)

First-party private **Kestrel-owned edits and RAW rights-cleared data** are a safer training foundation. Existing Lightroom parser/import may assist **interoperability**, but do NOT use Adobe-derived editing settings/outputs to train a commercial model until the contractual rights are reviewed. User's owned originals, RAW camera-noise calibration and new Kestrel-approved reversible edits are primary.

1. Participation is **separate, explicit, revocable opt-in**, never a condition of Pro, and never enabled by default. Offer clearly separate choices: (i) send minimal anonymous/aggregated model performance and accepted slider statistics; (ii) send selected *metadata-stripped* small crops and correction masks; (iii) submit selected full RAW/ground-truth pairs for high-quality training. People/photos can carry personal data; classify GPS, faces/children, unique landmarks, device serial, copyright and camera IDs, and require rights to share. Separate data controller/processor roles, purpose, retention, deletion rights, export and transparent privacy notice. Consult ICO guidance and DPIA counsel before launch.
2. Local-only personal preference learning should be usable with **zero upload**. Model edits belong to the user's private library by default. A user must explicitly select every image/crop uploaded for training; don't silently ship raw databases. Automatic upload of even seemingly anonymised full images is off the table. Avoid pretending a cryptographic hash is anonymisation when linkable.
3. Collection: versioned signed contributor consent receipt and dataset licence (ownership retained by photographer, specifically granted Kestrel training rights only when selected). User can view their submissions and withdraw future processing; implement raw-dataset deletion with retention log, discuss limits to removing effects from already trained weights instead of promising impossible model unlearning.
4. Backend: encrypted regional object storage with strict scoped credentials, queued integrity and malware/format screening, legal-data eligibility checks, dedup and private dataset registry. Weekly offline/queued **data curation/evaluation**, not unconditional paid GPU training. When enough lawful high-quality examples exist, run owner-approved budget-capped short Vertex AI/Cloud Run GPU job with reproducible training recipe, evaluation split, model/data card, cross-genre/age/skin/wildlife benchmark and regression policy; require signoff before signed release manifest. Never use third-party AI-produced reconstructions as unlicensed ground truth.
5. **Cost gates:** stage A metadata-only pilot with tiny panel; stage B selected crops and research proof; stage C larger rights-cleared RAW pairs only when benchmarks justify. Spending limit per week/model, alerts, job concurrency=0 outside runs, storage retention budgets and expected egress. Cloud cost must never become mandatory for everyday Free or Pro local use.
6. **Trust:** in-app plain English dashboard 'Stored only on this device / Selected for sharing / Sent / Deleted request'; privacy settings don't reset upon upgrade. Security risk/incident response and data-subject rights tracked before external launch.

## 4B. Colour Confidence — quiet, always-available photographic judgement (owner decision 21 November 2026)

**Product promise:** the editing experience has a calm, knowledgeable second set of eyes, especially useful to photographers who cannot confidently judge all hues by sight. **Never brand ordinary interactions "AI", add sparkle/magic-wand icons, or interrupt creativity with chatbot theatre.** Intelligence is infrastructure behind familiar controls: "Colour confidence", "Review colour", "Why?", "Compare", "Check now", "Keep my edit". It remains valuable and understandable to every genre and every photographer regardless of colour vision.

**USE WHAT WE ALREADY HAVE:** \`src/core/colourCheck.ts\` implements offline sRGB-preview palette, OKLab colour names/chroma/cast/clipping heuristic, original-vs-edit chroma change, subject palette and colour-vision deficiency simulation; \`src/core/aiReview.ts\` and the optional local OpenAI-compatible transport already propose bounded colour/light sliders. \`src/components/PhotoEditor.tsx\`, \`src/i18n/areas/colour/source.ts\`, \`src/i18n/areas/aiReview/source.ts\`, \`tests/colour-check.spec.ts\` are the current integration/wording tests. **Do NOT build parallel renderers, another colour engine or a new AI tab.** Current thumbnail-colour heuristics are *not* semantic species truth, camera/ICC fidelity or calibrated pixel-level diagnostics. Do not imply otherwise.

### A. When Colour Confidence observes
1. **Quietly as the user edits:** analyse a *stable completed render snapshot* after a brief idle/debounce (initial target 400–800 ms, tune to hardware); never per slider frame, or run heavy VLM inference on every pointer event. Work in bounded, low-priority cancellable background tasks; discard stale results by \`sourceContentHash + recipeVersion + renderColourProfile + subjectEvidenceVersion + modelVersion\` and stop on switch/undo/new edit. Cache accepted analysis; no source modification, no network. Keep sliders/zoom responsive.
2. **Review on demand:** "Check colour" available from editor's existing controls or Photo Info, with text-only plain-English explanation and optional comparison to before state. Also usable for any genre; no compulsory model download.
3. **At completion:** when user marks a photo finished / Finish My Shoot, run the same local review, preferring fresh high-resolution/provenance-aware data rather than an outdated 8-bit thumbnail.
4. **Before export:** offer unobtrusive "Colour checked" or "Review 1 colour concern" if the result is relevant and recent; check the **actual export colour pipeline/profile and selected recipe**, not only screen representation. Warnings are advisory and never block ordinary export unless the underlying renderer/ICC is actually invalid (existing source quality gate). No repeated modal or forced disclaimer; "Keep my edit" and "Why?" always available.
5. **Learning:** optional local acceptance/rejection of recommendations should tune personal preferences without silent uploads. Only explicit separately consented contribution can feed Kestrel model training. No training on proprietary third-party rendered/edit outputs without rights clearance.

### B. What Colour Confidence can and cannot claim
- **Measured, high-confidence findings:** numerical clipping/gamut, chroma increase relative to original, white balance/cast *where a defensible neutral anchor exists*, renderer/ICC mismatches, identifiable hue/saturation drift within a coherent region. Prefer full-precision pre-output and colour-profile-aware sampling when available; report the exact image/recipe/render version. An 8-bit preview can detect suspicious effects, **not prove accurate RAW colour**.
- **Plausibility, not truth:** a verified Eurasian Robin normally has an orange-red breast/face, olive/brown upperparts and pale belly; woodland greens should be botanically believable; clear daylight sky often blue. But plumage varies with age/subspecies/moult/light, leaves and sky vary with season/weather/lighting, and raw profiles, camera white balance, display and ambient lighting matter. No invented exact target RGB/colour ranges for species/foliage/sky and no absolute claim "the robin is accurate" merely from object ID. Only compare with rights-cleared, sourced *descriptive* reference knowledge and context with confidence. RSPB: https://www.rspb.org.uk/birds-and-wildlife/robin ; Cornell: https://www.allaboutbirds.org/guide/European_Robin/id ; ICC: https://www.color.org/displaycalibration/
- **Unknown is an acceptable result:** if subject identification, mask, illumination, neutral patch or camera/ICC profile is uncertain, state "I can't confirm this colour from this photo" or abstain. Never invent species confidence; don't silently neutralise golden hour, blue-hour, stage-light, deliberate warm/cool art, high-key portraits, colourful skies or unusual biological colour.
- **Safe positive wording:** "The foliage is more saturated than the original; you may prefer less", "The robin's breast is orange-red, which is typical", "No obvious colour clipping detected", "The blue sky has a noticeable cyan shift compared with before", "Colour appearance can't be certified without a reference/accurate profile." Avoid judgemental "wrong" when evidence only suggests a stylistic difference.
- **Visible accessibility by default:** named colours and spatial labels, icons/shapes/text not coloured-dot-only, no flashing/sparkle animation; screen-reader descriptions, touch targets, translations from British English, reduced-motion, consistent across desktop/tablet/mobile/foldable. The feature is valuable to everyone, not a separate "colour-blind mode" they must discover.

### B1. Make it tutorialistic without becoming a tutorial
- **Show cause and effect, not a lecture:** when a user strengthens Vibrance/Saturation or warms WB and Kestrel's measured regional result changes materially, a single discreet line can say, for example, "The background greens are now stronger than in the original" or "The robin's orange breast has shifted towards red." The message attaches to the editing control or existing Colour Confidence summary, rather than floating over the photograph.
- **Teach in context only:** "Why?" explains the observed relationship in one or two sentences ("Vibrance boosts softer colours. Saturation raises all colours, including already bright greens.") with a real before/after crop; a second optional 'Learn more' opens a small in-app help sheet. Never show unsolicited first-run tours, popup lectures, model chain-of-thought, pseudo confidence percentages or a chat transcript.
- **Respect individual vision:** describe colours by name and region, optional textual palette labels and luminance/contrast advice. Warn only where measured clipping, gamut risk or unusual shift is meaningful; no alarm for tasteful creative work. Support keyboard/screen-reader, voiceover labels and no dependence on green/red status cues.
- **Let the photographer lead:** after an explicit "Keep my look" or dismissal, suppress repetition on that photo/recipe until a material new change; no repeated notifications on every slider movement. Do not auto-apply suggestions. Explain if the observation was only a low-resolution preview estimate.

### C. Evidence hierarchy and models
1. **Layer 1: deterministic diagnostics**, always free/offline and fast: linear/high-bit channel statistics and output-space ICC/gamut where proven; regional hue/chroma/saturation change against before; gamut/clipping; neutral estimation only with confidence; subject/background/sky segmentation uncertainty; luminance contrast. This can run often.
2. **Layer 2: local visual understanding**, optional installed small quantised and RIGHTS-CLEARED model downloaded via section 4A signed cache: identify broad scene components, sky/foliage/skin/plumage and lighting, flag possible unnatural colours, understand intent. Run after edit settles or when asked, and only when device budget allows; no hidden cloud or model download. Reject/abstain on unknown and low-confidence. Validate outputs against deterministic measurements, never trust model language alone.
3. **Layer 3: locally cached, rights-cleared photographic knowledge**, short original-authored species/genre colour descriptions and tutorial principles with provenance/source/date. Never train on copyrighted tutorials without rights; reference text only if legally permitted. Respect lighting and real-world variability.
4. **Layer 4: optional bounded remedy**, only after user explicitly chooses it: gentle reversible colour, WB, vibrance/saturation, region mask adjustments in the SAME PhotoEdit recipe. Before/after preview and one-action Undo; never impose a correction or generate pixels. An optional "Suggest adjustment" uses the same 3-treatment Natural/Subject/Creative path from section 4A rather than a separate editor.

### D. Technical contracts
- Define \`ColourEvidence\` independent of UI: original vs rendered high-bit/ICC pipeline provenance; ROI segmentation identity and confidence; hue/chroma/luma distributions; before/after delta; subject/scene/illumination context; gamut/clipping; checks at requested output profile; risk/severity and **evidenceLevel** ('measured' | 'plausible' | 'unknown'), structured region and pixels only if legal/available. ## 4C. Natural subject masks and user-controlled guidance levels (owner decision 21 November 2026)

**Goal:** A selected bird must not look pasted onto the background; its visible legs, feet, fine feather edges and other believable anatomical parts should be included where observed. Transitions should be invisible at 100% and at export size. Exactly the same applies to hair, animal fur/paws, people, architecture and sporting subjects. **Natural photography, not artificial composites or hallucinated anatomy.** The intelligence quietly assists in the EXISTING editor; never build a permanent extra panel, sparkle/magic button, compulsory modal or AI chatbot.

### A. Existing foundations: extend, do not duplicate
- \`src/providers/subjectDetector.ts\` uses an optional verified U2NetP segmentation model, region-guided detection, a finer luminance-guided grid, and whole-photo fallback. \`src/core/maskTypes.ts\` persists normalized subject grids, optional detailed region, paint/erase strokes, feather and mask limits; \`src/core/masks.ts\` performs bilinear sampling, smoothstep softness and per-mask edit compositing, including intersect/subtract. \`src/components/MaskPanel.tsx\` already offers edge softness, paint/erase and refind; \`MaskOverlay.tsx\` overlays mask shapes. These are PARTIAL, not proof of complete legs or natural tonal transition.
- Own one versioned mask/recipe data contract and one renderer; strengthen source→preview→zoom→high-bit export parity. Avoid a parallel "smart masking engine" whose results cannot be edited or exported.
- Mask detection confidence is NOT anatomical completeness. An entire bird-shaped silhouette may omit feet/legs; global mask confidence alone cannot prove they are included.

### B. Two problems, two complementary corrections
1. **Segmentation completeness:** detect subject as a whole with calibrated category confidence; identify observable anatomy (bird body/head/wings/tail/legs/feet, mammal limbs, portrait hands/hair, etc.) through rights-cleared models and/or geometry/connected-component evidence; high-resolution subject-centred crops to recover thin parts. Compare candidate appendage segments with original RGB/RAW edges and foreground occlusion (branch, grass, perch). Accept only consistent observed pixels, abstain when anatomy is concealed or indistinguishable. Do NOT invent legs where none are visible, expand through background twigs, or treat branch/perch as an appendage. Allow 1-click Add missing area / Remove spill and existing brush correction. Preserve manual overrides and model version.
2. **Perceptual integration:** a correct alpha boundary can still look fake if subject exposure, clarity, sharpen, denoise or colour contrast differs too much from surroundings. Analyse a narrow *inside/outside boundary ring*, plus subject/background interior, at meaningful zoom. Evaluate local brightness/chroma discontinuity, sharpen/noise/texture halos, edge fringing, dark/light outlines, and background spill. Suggest or apply (depending on guidance mode and certainty) a small feather/radius/alpha-edge adjustment and/or reduced local adjustment strength rather than indiscriminately blurring the bird/legs. Prefer edge-aware guided/matting refinement, conservative dilation/erosion in physical pixel units, edge-protected smoothing and softly tapered influence. Perform compositing in true working colour/precision when supported; no visible seam in export; preserve texture and colour.
3. **Confidence and correction:** separate confidence fields for subject identity, region/appendage completeness, alpha-edge quality, foreground occlusion and proposed transition strength. A missing foot warning requires real observed evidence; if unresolved, "Review legs or feathers" not "Fixed legs". A 'strong cut-out look' is often an edit-strength issue; avoid applying more blurring when contrast adjustment should be lowered. All changes reversible and attributable to the confirmed same source/recipe/renderer version.
4. **Quality evaluation:** ground truth masks over rights-cleared real wildlife at varied poses, occluded birds on branches, leg/feet thin structures, feathers in sky, hair/wisps/people, fur/pets, sports equipment, foliage/architecture; alpha boundary metrics plus blind visual inspection of tone/noise/colour seam at Fit, 100%, 200%, and final 16-bit export where supported. Check no halo, unmasked body part, false attached branch, plastic fur, overly soft outline or spill. Report visible failures; don't count synthetic-only success as proof.

### C. User guidance — only ONE three-level setting

| Mode | Behaviour | Privacy and control |
|---|---|---|
| **Autopilot** | On new photos or edits, may apply ONLY high-confidence bounded reversible adjustments and mask blending/part corrections with a visible undo/history receipt and before/after. When anatomy/colour is uncertain, show a small 'Review' item instead, never force or invent. | Explicit opt-in from user; preserve manually locked edits and manual masks; never auto-delete, export or upload, never auto-apply cloud model output. No irreversibly moving files. |
| **Back-seat driver (recommended default)** | Quiet, debounced off-main-thread observation after render settles; one concise actionable hint at a time, if useful. Nothing applied without user approval. May signal mask missing feet, halo, too-strong colour, clipping, composition; no nagging or generic praise. | User can Apply / Show why / Ignore / Don't show for this image; respects dismissed notes and intentional looks. |
| **Only when asked** | No unsolicited observation, badges, toast, automatic VLM load or advice. Analyse only on Check now, Refine mask, Finish review if requested, or deliberate edit request. | Normal essential processing (RAW decode, renderer correctness, export validity) still works; nothing silently "improved". |

- Preference is easily found in existing **View** menu and central **Settings**, not duplicated as a constantly visible toolbar. Remember device defaults with optional project override and a per-photo temporary pause. Use existing \`Colour Confidence\` event queue from §4B for common checks; specific mask-refinement jobs share the bounded processing/task infrastructure from Worker 1. **Autopilot never changes a fully manual mask without clear explicit user-controlled permission**.
- No UI names such as "AI Magic", sparkles, wands, AI assistant dock or unrequested popups. Use plain British English: "Blend edges", "Refine subject", "Check colour", "Subject's legs may have been missed", "This local brightening leaves a visible edge", "Reduce effect", "Keep my edit".
