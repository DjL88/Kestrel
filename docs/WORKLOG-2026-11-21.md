7. **Secure and honest distribution:** restrict unnecessary filesystem/network privileges, protect BYO/connector credentials; assess codec/model license, sign/label downloadable artefacts appropriately; no unverified general-availability mobile, paid licence or professional DxO/Lightroom parity claims.

## 3. Competing bird culling product — SanjaySoniLV/ProjectKestrel

**Sources inspected directly (21 Nov 2026):**
- https://github.com/SanjaySoniLV/ProjectKestrel/blob/main/README.md
- https://github.com/SanjaySoniLV/ProjectKestrel/blob/main/AGENTS.md
- `analyzer/kestrel_analyzer/ml/speciesnet_sam_hq.py`, `ml/bioclip_species.py`, `ml/siglip.py`, `ml/quality.py`, `primary_subject.py`, `smart_search.py`, `ratings.py`, `analyzer/js/culling.js`.

This is an established **free, local-first bird-culling and catalogue app** (AGPLv3; Windows/Mac downloads; optional Perch outing-sharing and paid cloud compute). It is **not** a fully integrated RAW development/edit/export replacement; it opens an external editor and explicitly lists a limited quick editor as a future consideration. Product name/market overlap is severe and supports changing our brand prior to distribution. Do not assume trademark infringement merely from overlap; seek actual clearance.

### Their tangible technical advantages to benchmark, not copy

1. **Multi-bird detection + crop geometry:** current code uses MegaDetector proposal boxes and SAM-HQ segment masks. Historical SpeciesNet routing was replaced in pipeline 3 by BioCLIP 2 embedding-based bird vs wildlife routing, so don't cite SpeciesNet as the current primary species classifier.
2. **Global and regional species/family:** BioCLIP 2 zero-shot, IOC worldwide ~11,250 bird labels with a geofenced subset; separate family scores and user-reviewed corrections. The output is **relative to the allowed label set**; not guaranteed calibrated real-world correctness. Their README explicitly advises species-level caution.
3. **Image/subject sharpness ranking:** legacy CNN and optional calibrated SigLIP 2 embedding prompt pair for crop quality; rating thresholds and folder/percentile-normalised distribution. Their result is a bird-focused, subjective-review-friendly ranking, not aesthetic judgement.
4. **Subject continuity in bursts:** `primary_subject.py` includes motion-compensated bbox matching, BioCLIP/SigLIP embedding similarity, assignment with no-match and multi-target tracks across frames, plus confidence margins. Valuable benchmark for small/multiple birds and burst-by-burst pose choice.
5. **Contextual smart search:** SigLIP crop plus whole-frame embeddings with species/family constraints, enabling context queries such as 'bird on wire' versus crop-intrinsic terms.
6. **Photographer-facing usability:** side-by-side subject crop/full-frame, chronologically grouped scene timeline, adjustable accept/reject thresholds, review rejects before moving them to a designated folder, editable species labels, adjustable capture-time offsets, and XMP sidecars/optional JPEG metadata embedding.
7. **Runtime and ownership:** Python+pywebview+PyInstaller, local `.kestrel/` analysis outputs beside photos, DirectML/Core ML/CPU routes, local queues. Their optional cloud compute and Perch add-ons are outside their offline default.

### Gap re-ranking for *our* app

- **P0 safety:** bird/no-bird ambiguity, identity across photo changes, safe manual culling, no silent destructive rejection; subject crop must always match correct original.
- **P1 Select differentiation:** true-pixel *Subject Detail* crop/eye sharpness in single and A/B compare; per-bird continuity across bursts/multiple birds; noise/motion vs background sharpness; calibrated relative rank with uncertainty, adjustable keep-per-burst and user approval.
- **P1 bird ID:** model-based broad bird/family gate first, region-aware candidate list as a *prior* not certain truth, calibrated top candidates, clear unknown fallback, user-confirmed/manual tags and reliable family/species search. Evaluate independently with legal model/data rights; do not copy their AGPLv3 code, weights, prompts or proprietary output datasets.
- **P1 catalogue:** fast search across species/family and scene context, proper XMP export without default writes to original, reasonable shoot clock correction.
- **Our genuine differentiation:** complete import→high-bit RAW→reversible natural edit/masks→denoise→profiled export→offline reopen in ONE program, better calm accessible cross-genre UI, no compulsory external editor or subscription. The differentiator is only credible if basic source-to-output quality is proven.
- Do not chase Perch/social network or paid cloud jobs until core photographic work, privacy and reliability are established.
