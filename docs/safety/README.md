# Online safety paperwork: what to read, sign and keep

> **Drafts for the owner to check and sign. Not legal advice.** Updated 27 November 2026.

InFlight does not scan photos. Automated hash matching (PhotoDNA, IWF hash list), the Cloud Vision SafeSearch classifier, NCMEC CyberTipline reporting and evidence keeping were removed on 27 November 2026, because InFlight did not qualify for hash list access. Illegal content reaches a person only through reports.

## When child sexual abuse material is reported

1. In /admin → Safety, remove the photo or revoke the gallery. A child sexual abuse report already takes the gallery down at once.
2. Suspend the account.
3. Report it to the National Crime Agency (CEOP safety centre) and the Internet Watch Foundation (report.iwf.org.uk), with the gallery link and the report reference. Do not download, view more than needed, or keep copies.
4. Note the date and the NCA and IWF references in your records.
