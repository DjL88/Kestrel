# InFlight user guide — V2 workspace

This guide is being updated for InFlight's single photography workspace. The Projects section below describes the current V2 interface; the remaining panels will be documented separately. Some controls vary by device or licence.

## Projects

A project keeps your photographs, selection decisions and edits together. Your originals are not changed by editing. Keep a separate backup of the original files, even if you also back up the project.

1. From the Projects screen, choose **New project**.
2. Give the project a meaningful name and choose the photography project type, or choose the collage type for a layout project.
3. Select **Create project**. Alternatively, choose **Start from a folder instead** if you want to begin with a folder of photographs.
4. Open a project from the Projects screen to return to its workspace. Use the search field if you have several projects.

In the photography workspace, use **Import** to choose photographs. As the photographs become available, select one in the filmstrip to review it. Keep the source drive available; a preview is not a replacement for a missing original.

Before deleting a project or removing a photograph, read the confirmation carefully. Project decisions and edits are separate from the original files, but removing a project can still discard catalogue information.

## Earlier V1 workflow reference

The following instructions describe the earlier interface and are retained for reference until their V2 equivalents have been checked. Do not assume these menu labels, licence steps or recovery options match the current workspace.

### Unlock Kestrel Pro
1. Open Kestrel. On the home screen choose **Licence and settings** (bottom of the page).
2. Paste the licence key from your receipt email and choose **Unlock Kestrel Pro**.
3. That is all. No account, no sign-in, and it keeps working offline. Lost the key? Re-send it from your receipt.

### Keep your photos where you like
Kestrel never moves your originals. Keep them on any drive, a NAS, or in a folder that iCloud Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive or Google Drive syncs. If you move them later, drag the photos or their folder onto the Kestrel window and it reconnects them.

### Copy your settings to another device
Your shortcuts, macro pad, display choices, export presets and watermark can move with you. Photos, edits, licence and keys are never included.
1. On the first device: **Licence and settings**, then **Save settings file**. Save it in **iCloud Drive** (or Dropbox, OneDrive, Google Drive, or a USB stick).
2. On the second device: open Kestrel, **Licence and settings**, then **Load settings file**, and pick that file.
3. Kestrel reloads with your settings. Repeat after changing settings on either device.

Apple devices share iCloud Drive automatically, so step 1 on a Mac is all you do before step 2 on another Mac. Windows and Android can load the same file.
