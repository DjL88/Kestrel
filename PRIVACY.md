# InFlight privacy

The privacy notice lives on the website: [`site/privacy.html`](site/privacy.html), served at `/privacy`. The other policies are beside it: [`terms`](site/terms.html), [`acceptable-use`](site/acceptable-use.html), [`cookies`](site/cookies.html) and [`subprocessors`](site/subprocessors.html).

Keep those pages in step with the code. When a change adds a new kind of personal data, a new provider, a new cookie or browser storage key, or a new upload, update the matching page in the same PR.
