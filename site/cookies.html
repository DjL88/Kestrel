The short version

InFlight uses only storage that is strictly necessary for things you ask it to do. There are no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies, and no tracking pixels. Because of that, UK and EU law does not require a consent banner, and we do not show one. If that ever changes, we will ask before setting anything that is not strictly necessary.

Fonts and images on this site are served from our own address, and app downloads from our own Google Cloud storage, so no advertising or tracking company sees your visit.

Your choices

You can sign out, or clear this site's storage in your browser settings. Clearing storage removes locally saved projects and edits, so back up first. Your original photo files are never removed by clearing browser storage. See the privacy notice for the optional online features.
