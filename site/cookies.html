What we use

InFlight uses only strictly necessary cookies and similar storage to provide the features you request. We do not use analytics or advertising cookies, or tracking pixels.

  • Sign-in session: when you choose to sign in, session storage keeps you signed in and supports account security.
  • Local catalogue: browser or app storage keeps your projects, ratings and reversible edits available, including offline.
  • Settings: local storage remembers choices such as your language and workspace preferences.

Your choices

You can sign out or manage site storage in your browser settings. Clearing storage may remove locally saved projects and edits, so back up your catalogue first. Original photo files are not removed by clearing browser storage.

Optional connected services may have their own storage policies. See our privacy notice for details of optional online features.
