Installing the beta

Beta builds are not yet signed by Apple or Microsoft, so each system asks you to confirm before the first launch. Download only from this page or the Kestrel releases page.

macOS

Open the downloaded disk image and drag Kestrel to Applications. The first time, right-click Kestrel, choose Open, then Open again.

Windows

Run the installer. If Windows says it protected your PC, choose More info, then Run anyway.

Your photographs are never changed or uploaded by installing or using Kestrel. Signed installers will replace these steps in a later release.

Planned pricing

Nothing can be bought yet. Kestrel is a free beta. These are the plans, and they may change before anything goes on sale. No account or payment details are needed to use the beta.

Free beta Available now

Import, cull, edit and export on your own computer, offline. During the beta every feature is open.

Pro Planned, not on sale

Planned at £29 once, kept offline for good with a licence key and no account. It would keep masks, subject detection, species suggestions and assisted culling; Free would keep import, editing and export.

Cloud Later, not available

No cloud service exists. You keep your photos wherever you like, and can already copy settings between devices. A paid cloud option may follow if people ask for one.

System requirements

  • macOS: to be confirmed once the first beta is tested on real Macs (built for Apple silicon and Intel).
  • Windows: to be confirmed once the first beta is tested on real PCs.
  • Storage: room for the optional on-device species model (about 90 MB) and your catalogue. Originals can stay on external drives.

Getting started

  1. Import: choose photographs or a folder stored on your device. Keep a separate backup of your originals.
  2. Cull: review photographs, mark Keep or Reject, and compare neighbouring frames.
  3. Edit: adjust light, colour and crops. Edits are reversible and do not rewrite the original file.
  4. Export: choose a format and save the rendered photograph.

Nikon RAW note: Browser-based NEF handling currently relies on embedded previews. Full-resolution RAW decoding, including Nikon High Efficiency variants, must be validated in native builds before it is advertised as supported.

Your photos, your storage, your devices

  1. Unlock Pro: on the home screen choose Licence and settings and paste the key from your receipt. No account, and it works offline.
  2. Keep photos anywhere: on any drive, a NAS, or a folder that iCloud Drive, Dropbox or OneDrive syncs. Kestrel never moves or changes your originals.
  3. Copy settings to another device: choose Save settings file and save it in iCloud Drive, then choose Load settings file on your other Mac, PC or phone.

Your photographs remain yours

Kestrel is local-first. Import, cull and edit without an account. Optional online analysis or future cloud features must be turned on deliberately. Your originals are not silently uploaded.
