What's inside

Smart culling

Bursts and look-alike frames are grouped so you compare them side by side. On recent Nikon Z bodies it also draws a box on the photo where the camera focused. You make the call, and any pick can be undone.

Subject identification

Names bird species and tags aircraft and vehicles, so you can search a shoot by what is in it. When it isn't sure, it says so.

Social formats

Set up the crops and sizes you post at, once. One edit then exports to every format, each with its own crop.

ColourSense

Colour notes measured from the photo itself, not your screen, and told in plain words. Colour-blind photographers can edit with confidence.

Masking, before and after, denoise

Adjust just part of a photo, compare before and after, and clean up noise. Nothing is generated: no invented pixels.

Custom overlays and watermarks

Save your logo, colours and text style as a brand kit, and Export puts them on every photo in one step.

Installing InFlight

Early builds are not yet signed by Apple or Microsoft, so each system asks you to confirm before the first launch. Download only from this page.

macOS

Open the downloaded disk image and drag InFlight to Applications. The first time, right-click InFlight, choose Open, then Open again.

Windows

Run the installer. If Windows says it protected your PC, choose More info, then Run anyway.

Your photographs are never changed or uploaded by installing or using InFlight. Signed installers will replace these steps in a later release.

System requirements

  • macOS: to be confirmed once the first build is tested on real Macs (built for Apple silicon and Intel).
  • Windows: to be confirmed once the first build is tested on real PCs.
  • Storage: room for the optional on-device species model (about 90 MB) and your catalogue. Originals can stay on external drives.

Your photos, your storage, your devices

  1. Keep photos anywhere: on any drive, a NAS, or a folder that iCloud Drive, Dropbox or OneDrive syncs. InFlight never moves or changes your originals.
  2. Copy settings to another device: choose Save settings file and save it in iCloud Drive, then choose Load settings file on your other Mac, PC or phone.

Your photographs remain yours

InFlight is local-first. Import, cull and edit without an account. Optional online analysis or future cloud features must be turned on deliberately. Your originals are not silently uploaded.
