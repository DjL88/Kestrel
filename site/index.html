More time behind the camera.

Kite44 is a photo workspace for importing, culling, editing and exporting. It sets out to make editing quicker and easier to understand: find the keepers, make a few edits you can follow, and export.

What Kite44 sets out to do

Work offline

Import, cull, edit and export without an internet connection or an account. Your photos stay on your device or in storage you choose.

Never change your originals

Kite44 does not change, move or replace an original file. Edits are saved as steps you can change or undo at any time.

Stay calm and accessible

A quiet screen, plain words, full keyboard access and reduced motion. Nothing relies on colour alone. It is designed with colour-blind, ADHD and autistic photographers in mind.

Be honest about what it knows

When Kite44 is unsure, it says so. It tells you when it shows a RAW file’s built-in preview, never invents detail, and keeps uncertain photos for you to review instead of rejecting them.

Help without taking over

Machine learning can help reduce noise, find a subject or suggest a mask. Every suggestion is yours to check and adjust.

Work the same everywhere

The same tools and the same way of working on Windows, macOS and the web, with iPhone, iPad and Android to follow.

Pay once

Basic stays free. Pro is a one-off licence that works offline, not a subscription. Invited testers can receive Pro at no charge.

Installing Kite44

Early builds are not yet signed by Apple or Microsoft, so each system asks you to confirm before the first launch. Download only from this page.

macOS

Open the downloaded disk image and drag Kite44 to Applications. The first time, right-click Kite44, choose Open, then Open again.

Windows

Run the installer. If Windows says it protected your PC, choose More info, then Run anyway.

Your photographs are never changed or uploaded by installing or using Kite44. Signed installers will replace these steps in a later release.

System requirements

  • macOS: to be confirmed once the first build is tested on real Macs (built for Apple silicon and Intel).
  • Windows: to be confirmed once the first build is tested on real PCs.
  • Storage: room for the optional on-device species model (about 90 MB) and your catalogue. Originals can stay on external drives.

Getting started

  1. Import: choose photographs or a folder stored on your device. Keep a separate backup of your originals.
  2. Cull: review photographs, mark Keep or Reject, and compare neighbouring frames.
  3. Edit: adjust light, colour and crops. Edits are reversible and do not rewrite the original file.
  4. Export: choose a format and save the rendered photograph.

Nikon RAW note: Browser-based NEF handling currently relies on embedded previews. Full-resolution RAW decoding, including Nikon High Efficiency variants, must be validated in native builds before it is advertised as supported.

Your photos, your storage, your devices

  1. Unlock Pro: on the home screen choose Licence and settings and paste the key from your receipt. No account, and it works offline.
  2. Keep photos anywhere: on any drive, a NAS, or a folder that iCloud Drive, Dropbox or OneDrive syncs. Kite44 never moves or changes your originals.
  3. Copy settings to another device: choose Save settings file and save it in iCloud Drive, then choose Load settings file on your other Mac, PC or phone.

Your photographs remain yours

Kite44 is local-first. Import, cull and edit without an account. Optional online analysis or future cloud features must be turned on deliberately. Your originals are not silently uploaded.
