Licensees and devices

Revoking a key stops new activations and renewals. It never touches anyone's photos or edits, and a device keeps its offline grant until it lapses (30 days). Freeing a device lets the key be used on another one.

Revoke a key by pasting it

For a key that leaked, including one nobody has used yet (such keys are not in the list above). Only its licence id is kept; the key itself is not stored. Devices that check online turn Pro off; their photos and edits are untouched.

Grant free Pro access by email

No licence key or payment is needed. Access attaches to the recipient's verified account when they sign in with this address. Repeating a grant for the same person and major version does not create another licence.

Issue a free Pro tester or gift key

Switches

One on/off switch for each managed feature. New features start off and only work once the service is set up for them and you switch them on. Switching off takes effect at once and is written to the audit log. Switching off never stops people reading or deleting their own data. Managed Gemini and cloud processing count against the monthly spending limit.

Feature flags

Switch a feature on or off for everyone, for a plan, for an organisation or for one person, over a date range. A trial is a rule with a date range; leave the last day empty and it runs until you stop it. The most specific rule wins (one person, then an organisation, then a plan, then everyone), and an off rule beats an on rule at the same level. Changes reach a device the next time it checks in, and are written to the audit log.
