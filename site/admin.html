        For a key that leaked, including one nobody has used yet (such keys are not in the list above). Only its licence id is kept; the key itself is not stored. Devices that check online turn Pro off; their photos and edits are untouched.

        Feature flags

        Switch a feature on or off for everyone, for a plan, for an organisation or for one person, over a date range. A trial is a rule with a date range; leave the last day empty and it runs until you stop it. The most specific rule wins (one person, then an organisation, then a plan, then everyone), and an off rule beats an on rule at the same level. Changes reach a device the next time it checks in, and are written to the audit log.
