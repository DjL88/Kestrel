Using InFlight

InFlight is a local-first photography workspace. Use it only with photographs and other material you are entitled to use. You retain the rights you have in your photographs; editing does not transfer ownership to InFlight.

Original photographs and backups

InFlight is designed to preserve originals and keep editing information separately. Keep independent backups of original photographs and project data. A preview does not replace an original, and a missing source may prevent full-resolution export.

Basic, Pro and accounts

Basic is intended to remain free. Pro is planned as a one-off licence. The public website does not currently take payments. Final prices, licence scope, supported devices, refunds and purchase terms must be confirmed before sales open. Some optional services may need an account and connection.

Optional online services

Local editing does not require an account or online helper. Optional connected storage, account services or photographic advice may have separate provider terms and availability. Review the relevant choices and the privacy page before enabling them.

Pre-release features and help

Features may be incomplete or change during testing. Check the results of automated selections and suggested edits before export. Report issues through the contact section.
