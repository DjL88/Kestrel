The short version

  • InFlight is local-first. You can import, cull, edit and export photographs with no account and no internet connection.
  • Your original photos are never changed in place and are never uploaded unless you switch on a feature that says it uploads, and choose to use it.
  • No advertising, no data sale, no tracking cookies and no analytics in the app or on this site.
  • If you make an account, buy Pro, use cloud edits or cloud storage, or send us a message, we hold a small amount of data about you. This page lists all of it, why, for how long, and how to get a copy or have it deleted.

What stays on your device

  • Your original photos. They stay where you keep them. InFlight stores links, previews and reversible edit recipes beside them.
  • Your catalogue: projects, ratings, flags, crops, tags, masks and edit history.
  • On-device suggestions. Culling, subject and species suggestions, auto-edits and other built-in models run on your device. Your photos are not sent anywhere to produce them.
  • Your settings, your licence, and the sign-in details for any storage you connect yourself.

We cannot see any of this, so we cannot export or delete it for you. You can do both yourself; see Your rights.

We do not use your data for automated decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you. We do not knowingly process special category data. Your photos may show other people; when you share them through InFlight, you decide who sees them.

Features where your data goes to someone else you choose

These features talk straight from your device to a service you pick. We are not involved and never see the data:

  • Your own Gemini key. A downscaled copy of the photo, with metadata removed, goes from your device to Google using your key, under your agreement with Google.
  • Your own cloud storage. S3-compatible storage, WebDAV, Dropbox, Google Drive or OneDrive. InFlight never sees your password; sign-in tokens and the details you type stay on your device.
  • Model downloads. Fixed, checksum-verified files. Nothing about you or your photos is sent to fetch them.
  • Feature settings. At most once a day the app fetches a small signed file saying which features are on. The request carries no identifier and no cookies.

Who processes data for us

We use a small number of service providers to run InFlight's online parts. They act on our instructions and may not use your data for their own purposes. The current list, with what each does and where, is on the subprocessors page. We do not sell or rent personal data to anyone, and we do not share it for advertising.

We may disclose data if the law requires it, for example to report child sexual abuse material we are made aware of to the authorities (see the acceptable use policy), or to protect people from harm.

International transfers

Cloud photo storage, client galleries, the cloud runner and our licence database are in Google Cloud's London region. Some providers process data outside the UK and EEA, mainly in the United States: Firebase Authentication, Firebase Hosting's global network, Google's Gemini model (only if you use the managed second opinion) and Paddle's payment processing. Where data leaves the UK or EEA, we rely on adequacy regulations (including the UK–US data bridge and EU–US Data Privacy Framework where the provider is certified) or on the standard contractual clauses and UK addendum built into the provider's data processing terms.

Your rights, and how to use them

Under UK and EU data protection law you have the right to access your data, to have it corrected, to have it erased, to restrict or object to how we use it, to get a copy in a portable form, and to withdraw consent at any time. We answer within one month. It is free unless a request is plainly unfounded or excessive.

Choose or stop the messages we send

  • In the app: signed in, open Settings, then Account, then Communication preferences. There are separate switches for InFlight news by email (marketing, off unless you turn it on), emails about your shared projects (on unless you turn them off), and alerts: on this device, and which kinds on all your devices.
  • From an email: every email has a link at the bottom, and a one-click unsubscribe your email app can show. In a news email it stops news; in a shared-project email it stops those. Either can be turned back on in the app. You can also reply with “unsubscribe”.
  • Account emails, such as your licence key or the email-address check and password reset that Firebase sends, are part of the service and are not optional while you have an account.
  • Whatever you switch off, notifications still appear inside the app, so you miss nothing in your shared projects.

Get a copy of your data

  • On your device: in InFlight, use Back up now to save a complete project backup, or save XMP sidecars from the File menu. Exported photos are ordinary files.
  • Cloud photos: download them from the app at any time, including during the 90-day read-only period after a plan ends.
  • What we hold about you: write to hello@inflight.studio from the email address you signed in with, or use the contact form and say “data copy”. We send your account, device, purchase, shared-project, message and communication-preference records as a machine-readable file.

Erase and forget me

  • On your device: open Settings, then Privacy, then Erase this device. It removes every project, edit, preview and saved choice InFlight keeps in that browser or app. Your original photo files are never touched. It needs no account or connection.
  • Opt-ins: switching off “help improve suggestions” deletes what you shared. Switching off the managed second opinion stops it straight away. Turning off news emails removes the record of your agreement.
  • Your account and everything on our servers: in the app, open Settings, then Account, and choose Delete my account. Or write to us as above and say “delete my account”, and we check the request comes from the account's email address. Your account, devices, shared projects you own (the people you share with are told), cloud copies and messages are deleted 30 days after you ask, unless you sign in and keep the account before then. We tell you what was deleted and what the law makes us keep, such as purchase records. Any unused cloud balance is refunded to you through Paddle. Download my data, in the same place, gives you a copy first.

US state privacy rights

If you live in California or another US state with a consumer privacy law, you can ask to know, get a copy of, correct or delete your personal information in the same way. We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising, and we do not use sensitive personal information to infer characteristics about you. We will not treat you differently for using these rights.

Complaints

Please tell us first so we can put it right. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint, or to the data protection authority where you live or work.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Cloud objects are private and opened only through short-lived signed links. Links to client galleries are stored as one-way hashes. Secrets stay in a secrets manager, never in the app or the code. If a breach puts your data at risk, we will tell you and the ICO as the law requires.

Children

InFlight is not aimed at children. You must be 13 or over to use InFlight on your own device, and 18 or over to create an account, share, use cloud storage or buy. Without an account, InFlight works only on your own device and sends us nothing about you. If you think a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this notice

We will update this page when InFlight changes how it uses data, change the date at the top, and tell account holders by email or in the app before any change that matters.
